Chart from table data

Turn this on to build a bar or column chart from one of your table's columns. It's pure HTML with inline styles (no images or scripts), so it pastes like the table and updates as you edit.

Unstyled mode: the chart is left out of the HTML output, because a chart can't be drawn without styling. Use Copy chart HTML (which keeps its own inline styles) or the image exports.
